\section*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Preface}
This thesis was prepared at DTU Compute, the Department of Applied Mathematics
and Computer Science at the Technical University of Denmark, in partial
fulfilment of the requirements for the degree of Bachelor of Science in
Artificial Intelligence and Data. The work was carried out in the spring of 2026
under the supervision of Finn \AA{}rup Nielsen.

The thesis develops and evaluates an end-to-end system for day-ahead electricity
price forecasting, pairing the benchmark forecasting models of the literature
with the MLOps practices needed to operate them in production. It assumes the
reader is familiar with the basics of machine learning and applied statistics.
